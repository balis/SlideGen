Title: Learning under Concept Drift: A Review
Authors: Jie Lu, Anjin Liu, Fan Dong, Feng Gu, João Gama, Guangquan Zhang
Published: 2020 (IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering)
Retrieved: 2026-06-15

FULL EXCERPT (from search result content summarizing the paper's taxonomy):
"Real concept drifts happen when the relation between the input and the target changes:
P_t(y|x) ≠ P_{t+1}(y|x) whilst P_t(x) = P_{t+1}(x)."

"Virtual drift is drift in P(x) without affecting P(y|x), also known as covariate shift or
dataset shift."

"Since the joint probability P_t(X, y) can be decomposed into two parts as
P_t(X, y) = P_t(X) × P_t(y|X), concept drift can be triggered by three sources:
(1) P_t(X) ≠ P_{t+1}(X) while P_t(y|X) = P_{t+1}(y|X) — covariate drift;
(2) P_t(y|X) ≠ P_{t+1}(y|X) while P_t(X) = P_{t+1}(X) — real concept drift;
(3) both changing together."
