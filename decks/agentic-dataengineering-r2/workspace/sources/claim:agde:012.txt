EXCERPT (supporting claim:agde:drift-taxonomy:006):
"Covariate shift: P(X) changes, but P(Y|X) remains the same."
"Concept drift: P(Y|X) changes, but P(X) remains the same."
"Example of concept drift: Before COVID-19, a San Francisco apartment cost $2M; after, the same apartment costs $1.5M despite identical features."
"Data shifts happen all the time, suddenly, gradually, or seasonally. A model does great when first deployed, but its performance degrades over time as the data distribution changes."

"Data drift (also known as covariate shift) refers to the phenomenon where the distribution of data inputs that an ML model was trained on differs from the distribution of the data inputs that the model is applied to. Critically, in pure covariate shift the concept P(Y|X) remains the same while the probability distribution of inputs P(X) changes."
