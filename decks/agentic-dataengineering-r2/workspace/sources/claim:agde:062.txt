EXCERPTS (fetched from https://arxiv.org/html/2402.07867v3):
"An attacker could inject a few malicious texts into the knowledge database of a RAG system to induce an LLM to generate an attacker-chosen target answer."

"The system achieves a 97% ASR by injecting 5 malicious texts for each target question into a knowledge database (with 2,681,468 clean texts) in black-box settings on the Natural Questions dataset."

"Under white-box conditions with multiple datasets, ASRs consistently exceed 90%."

"The knowledge database in a RAG system introduces a new and practical attack surface."

"The attack decomposes malicious texts into two components: a Generation Component (I) crafted to satisfy the generation condition — ensuring the LLM produces the target answer when given this text as context — and a Retrieval Component (S) designed to satisfy the retrieval condition — making the malicious text retrievable for target questions."

"Attackers can inject malicious content through various means — Wikipedia editing, fake news posts, or compromising enterprise knowledge bases — without needing direct access to the LLM or retriever components."
