STATEMENT: According to the CrowdFlower 2016 Data Science Survey, 80% of data scientists' work consists of data preparation: 60% of respondents spent most of their time cleaning and organizing data, and 19% spent most of their time collecting data sets.

EXCERPT:
"According to a 2016 survey by CrowdFlower, as much as 80% of the work of data scientists consists of data preparation, including 19% of time spent on collecting data sets and 60% on cleaning and organising data. 3 out of every 5 data scientists surveyed actually spend the most time cleaning and organizing data."

CORROBORATING EVIDENCE:
The Anaconda State of Data Science 2021 survey found data scientists reported spending 39% of their time specifically on data preparation and data cleansing — more than on model training, model selection, and deployment combined (source: https://www.anaconda.com/state-of-data-science-2021). The 2022 Anaconda survey found 37.75% on data prep vs 26.44% on model work. The decline from ~80% to ~39% reflects improved tooling, but data tasks still dominate.

CAVEAT:
The 80% figure is the most widely cited (from CrowdFlower 2016) but the precise split between "data layer" tasks depends on definition. More recent surveys show lower figures (39-45% per Anaconda 2020-2022), suggesting the "70-80%" framing in the objective is best attributed to the CrowdFlower-era surveys and should be qualified as a commonly-cited historical benchmark rather than a current measurement.
