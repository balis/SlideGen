Title: Drowning in Documents: Consequences of Scaling Reranker Inference
Venue: Accepted to ReNeuIR 2025 Workshop at SIGIR 2025
Year: 2024 (published November 2024)

KEY FINDINGS (from web search summaries of the paper):
"while rerankers initially help with small values for K, reranking with large K decreases recall precipitously, often dropping beneath the quality of standalone retrievers."

"In some cases, when evaluated in a fair competition—ranking the full dataset—rerankers are less accurate than retrievers."

"The efficacy of two-stage retrieval pipelines is limited by the recall ceiling of the first stage, as no amount of sophisticated reranking can recover relevant documents that the initial bi-encoder failed to retrieve."

"rerankers hit a recall ceiling when dealing with large numbers of documents retrieved in the first stage."

"The paper finds that the common assumptions about rerankers are frequently false. Specifically, while rerankers initially help with small values for K, reranking with large K decreases recall precipitously."

The researchers tested best-in-class rerankers across several public and enterprise IR benchmarks, using datasets and models that depart from conventional reranker research focused on BM25 + MSMarco.
