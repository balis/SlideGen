"Access Controls: Implement fine-grained access permissions for embeddings and vector databases."

ADDITIONAL CONTEXT (from OWASP LLM06 search summaries):
OWASP breaks LLM06 Excessive Agency into three root causes:
1. Excessive functionality — agents can reach tools beyond their task scope
2. Excessive permissions — tools operate with broader privileges than necessary
3. Excessive autonomy — high-impact actions proceed without human oversight

"Extensions having permissions on downstream systems that are not needed, such as an extension intended to read data connecting with UPDATE, INSERT and DELETE permissions."

"Applications failing to independently verify and approve high-impact actions."
