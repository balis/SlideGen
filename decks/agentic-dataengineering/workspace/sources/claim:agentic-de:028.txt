Title: Do Offline Metrics Predict Online Performance in Recommender Systems?
Authors: Karl Krauth, Sarah Dean, Alex Zhao, Wenshuo Guo, Mihaela Curmei, Benjamin Recht, Michael I. Jordan
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.07931
Retrieved: 2026-06-14

Excerpt:
"Offline metrics are correlated with online performance over a range of environments."

"However, improvements in offline metrics lead to diminishing returns in online performance."

"The ranking of recommenders varies depending on the amount of initial offline data available."

The study evaluated eleven recommenders across six controlled simulated environments to investigate the extent to which offline metrics predict online performance. The paper was submitted November 7, 2020.
