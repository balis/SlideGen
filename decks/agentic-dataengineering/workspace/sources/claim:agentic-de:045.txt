Title: Reciprocal Rank Fusion outperforms Condorcet and Individual Rank Learning Methods
Authors: Gordon V. Cormack, Charles L. A. Clarke, Stefan Buettcher
URL/DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/1571941.1572114
Venue: SIGIR 2009, Proceedings of the 32nd International ACM SIGIR Conference, pp. 758-759
Retrieved: 2026-06-14

Excerpt:
"Reciprocal Rank Fusion is a simple method for combining the document rankings from multiple IR systems. RRF consistently yields better results than any individual system, and better results than the standard method Condorcet Fuse."

The RRF formula scores each document d as: RRFscore(d) = sum over all ranking lists R of 1/(k + r_R(d)), where r_R(d) is the rank of document d in list R and k is a constant (typically 60). The method requires no score normalisation, operating purely on rank positions.
