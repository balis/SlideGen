Title: Building Effective Agents
Author: Anthropic
Published: December 2024

Excerpt:
Anthropic strongly advises "finding the simplest solution possible, and only increasing complexity
when needed." For many applications, "optimizing single LLM calls with retrieval and in-context
examples is usually enough" without building full agentic systems.

Anthropic's five workflow design patterns — Prompt Chaining, Routing, Parallelization,
Orchestrator-Worker, Evaluator/Optimizer — offer composable building blocks. The overarching
principle is that "success means building the right system for your needs, not the most
sophisticated one."

The framework names three building blocks — augmented LLM, five workflow patterns, and the
autonomous agent loop — that decompose "build an agent" into a structured selection problem,
positioning agents as composable nodes within larger orchestrated systems rather than as
unconstrained end-to-end orchestrators.
