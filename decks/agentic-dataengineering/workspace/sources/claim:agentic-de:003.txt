Title: The AI Hierarchy of Needs
Author: Monica Rogati
Published: June 2017, HackerNoon / Medium
URL: https://hackernoon.com/the-ai-hierarchy-of-needs-18f111fcc007

Excerpt:
"Think of AI as the top of a pyramid of needs. Yes, self-actualization (AI)
is great, but you first need food, water and shelter (data literacy,
collection and infrastructure)."

The pyramid has five layers from bottom to top:
1. Data collection (instrumentation, logging, sensors, external data, user-generated content)
2. Data flow / infrastructure (reliable ETL, pipelines, storage)
3. Exploration / transformation (cleaning, anomaly detection, preparation)
4. Analytics / features (A/B testing, metrics, segments, aggregates, features, training data)
5. AI / Deep Learning (the top of the pyramid)
