Excerpt:
"A model that looked great offline can mask a latency regression that drives users away."

"In fraud detection, offline recall improves dramatically when risky transactions come with confirmed fraud signals, but in production, the model is supposed to never see the future."

"Offline datasets are always approximations of production. They may underrepresent edge cases, omit new user behaviors, or reflect historical patterns that have already shifted."

"A model can look improved offline and still cause new issues in real usage, especially if the deployment environment is noisy or user inputs change over time."
