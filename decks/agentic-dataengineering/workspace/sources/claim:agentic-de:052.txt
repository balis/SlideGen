Additional context from related search (vellum.ai, Six Maturity Levels of AI Agents):
The maturity framework defines levels from L0 (None) through L5 (Full), with progressively
increasing autonomy and changing human roles from Integrating (L1) to Supervising (L3) to
Onlooker (L4). Most enterprise AI systems still sit at L1 and L2 with focus on orchestration,
while L3 and L4 are still hard for general-purpose agents because most production workflows
still need a human in the loop somewhere.
