Excerpt:
"Almost the same percentage of respondents reported they spent most of their
time cleaning data (60 percent) as those who reported that task to be the
least enjoyable part of their job (57 percent)."

An additional 19 percent of time was reported spent on collecting data sets,
bringing the total data preparation effort to approximately 79 percent.

Note: The "80% of time on data" figure widely cited in industry originates
from this 2016 CrowdFlower survey of approximately 80 data scientists.
