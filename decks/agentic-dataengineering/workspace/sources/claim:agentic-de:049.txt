Title: Building Effective Agents
Author: Anthropic
Published: December 2024

Excerpt:
"Workflows are systems where LLMs and tools are orchestrated through predefined code paths.
Agents are systems where LLMs dynamically direct their own processes and tool usage, maintaining
control over how they accomplish tasks."

Agents operate "in a loop" where they "use tools based on environmental feedback." The document
emphasizes that agents should include "stopping conditions (such as a maximum number of iterations)
to maintain control."

Anthropic strongly advises: "finding the simplest solution possible, and only increasing complexity
when needed."
