Excerpt:
"Two-stage retrieval separates candidate generation (fast, broad search) from ranking (slow, precise evaluation). Your initial retrieval needs to be fast and cast a wide net. Your reranker needs to be accurate and selective."

"Cross-encoders process query and document together, capturing interactions between them for more accurate relevance scoring."

Supporting source (Pinecone): "A reranking model — also known as a cross-encoder — is a type of model that, given a query and document pair, will output a similarity score. A reranker is used to rerank those documents retrieved by the first-stage model." (https://www.pinecone.io/learn/series/rag/rerankers/)
