Title: Data Versioning: ML Best Practices Checklist 2026
URL: https://labelyourdata.com/articles/machine-learning/data-versioning

Excerpt:
"Managing and versioning data correctly is essential for reproducibility and
traceability within ML pipelines. Modern frameworks automatically track and
version every artifact — data, models, or evaluations — upon pipeline
execution, adopting a data-first approach where all artifacts are versioned
and identified by content hash."

Additional context from search results:
Some frameworks (e.g., HP CMF, DVC, lakeFS) adopt a data-first approach
where all artifacts are versioned and identified by content hash, bringing
Git-like semantics to data — allowing engineers to branch, commit, and roll
back datasets just as they would with code. This contrasts with BI's
approach of querying the current state of a live table.
