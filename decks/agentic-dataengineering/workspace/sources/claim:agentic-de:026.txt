Title: Difference between KL Divergence and PSI
Author: Shrutika Poyrekar

Retrieved: 2026-06-14

Excerpt:
"PSI has a number of advantages compared to KL divergence for troubleshooting data model comparisons because if you swap the baseline distribution p(x) and sample distribution q(x), you will get the same number."

"KL Divergence and PSI are the two metrics which are commonly used to monitor data drift in model monitoring."

PSI is the Jeffreys divergence (symmetric KL), which resolves the asymmetry problem of raw KL divergence where D_KL(P||Q) ≠ D_KL(Q||P).
