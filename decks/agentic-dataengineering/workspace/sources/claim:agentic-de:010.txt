Title: Point-in-Time Correct Joins — Hopsworks MLOps Dictionary
URL: https://www.hopsworks.ai/dictionary/point-in-time-correct-joins
Section: Definition and rationale

Excerpt:
"A point-in-time correct join is a temporal join between two tables where the output reflects the state of both tables at a specific point in time defined by timestamps from the left table. In SQL, a PiT Join is an ASOF LEFT JOIN. The AS OF join ensures that the most recent value of the feature at the time of the timestamp is used in the training set."
